## Decisions I took for you (each one a setting or a line you can overturn)

- **Shadow mode is OFF** — the impact system is live from the first battle. One checkbox (*Shadow mode*) turns every
  effect into "log what would happen" if you want to watch first. (DESIGN decision 40)
- **Your unfinished sentence** ("also add the damage dealt be able to…"): `ImpactDamageWeight` stays **0** until you
  tell me what you meant. (IMPACT §8 q1)
- **A heavy right-side shield** between two empty men gives 76% stagger / 35% knockdown instead of your 80 / 40 —
  the shield's weight counts; say the word and the right side is exactly 80 / 40. (decision 43)
- **The block stun has no dice** — a tired block freezes longer by a fixed amount; the randomness lives in the stagger roll. (43)
- **Riders' shields can drop** (kicks, bashes, wrong-side blocks), at the same chance as on foot. (45)
- **Rider momentum is half a man's**: +30% at a gallop vs +60% sprinting on foot; a **couched lance gets nothing**
  (it is already the game's big hit). A cavalry charge throws a tired line down, never the whole line. (45)
- **No slowed get-up after being knocked off a horse** — the saddle fall is the game's own (unproven lever). (45)
- **On-screen lines: only yours by default** (what happens to you, what you do); your side's heroes behind
  *your side's heroes too*. Never a line in shadow mode. One line per 1.5 s, merged. (45)
- **Dropped shields** are picked up the vanilla way (`ShieldPickupLockSeconds` 0). (42)
- **Courage**: riders, men held in a shield wall, a siege's attackers and tournaments are not looked at; a man in his
  fever presses whatever the odds unless he was hit; a timid man surges in about once in 10 s when fresh. (41)
- **The punish**: the AI's own decision is made eager (100% of the game's AI), nothing forced; checked every ~0.25 s;
  only men in contact; a man who was just hit still presses — only a panicking man stays away. (46)
- **ALT bars**: the enemy's shown by default; one cap of 200 men marked (the nearest on screen). (38)

## What "broken" looks like

- Any `[error]` line (the game also shows a red "an error was caught and logged" line) — the mod falls back to
  vanilla for that spot and keeps going; send me the line with its stack.
- A man frozen, sliding, or a rider / horse playing a stagger animation (must never happen).
- A stutter in big battles — the `cost` numbers in the summary lines tell me which part.
- ON = OFF in the punish proof (the AI ignores the raised eagerness in a short window — my fallback is a direct
  attack wish through the input hook).

## Known risks

- **Levers only the game can prove.** The offline smoke plays the engine with stand-ins. Whether the engine really
  plays a KnockDown on a kick, a forced stagger, a slowed reaction, our own knockdown blow, a dismount from our answer
  or our `CanDismount` blow, and whether the native AI reacts to a raised decision chance within a 1-2 s window — that
  is exactly what tests 1, 2, 3 and 6 answer. Test 1 first: a FAILS there changes what the rest means.
- **Balance.** Many brakes now stack on a tired man (fewer decisions, the pause, bracing, courage) while the impact
  system punishes him harder. Watch for deadlocks between two spent lines, or the opposite — a tired line collapsing
  too fast once a few men stagger (*Punish: attack eagerness* 80 or the grace 0 are the gentle knobs).
- **`GetTargetAgent()`** is assumed to be the man an AI soldier fights (`impact punish windows … seen` tells).
- **Smart App Control** blocked freshly built test DLLs a few times during the night's builds (per file hash); a new
  commit and rebuild cleared it every time. If a deploy or the game refuses the dev DLL, tell me — I never change
  Windows security settings.
